upgrade thumbnail quality
It is disabled by default because: - It'll load the high quality
thumbnail as well as whatever thumbnail Youtube loads which is extra
data. - High quality thumbnails makes animations feel stuttery, the
degree varies with which TV you have. - It doesn't replace thumbnails
from A/B tests so it'll look jarring having high quality and low quality
thumbnails side by side.
