mouxon 08/01/2023 9:53 AM
Hi, The 2 files in ipks (just renamed the direcoty to ipks-official) are
the ipk provided by LG for my TV (LG G1). If these 2 ipks doesn't work,
it means your TV is using a different architecture. What you will need:
the official youtube IPK provided for your TV and the architecture used
to build it, so can build Cobalt and your IPK for the same architecture.
There are 3 arm architecture available on cobalt: arm-softfp, arm-hardfp
and arm64. I am using arm-softfp Once you got, your offical ipk, you can
try:
make OFFICAL_YOUTUBE_IPK=./your-official-youtube.ipk COBALT_ARCHITECTURE=arm-hardfp
or
make OFFICAL_YOUTUBE_IPK=./your-official-youtube.ipk COBALT_ARCHITECTURE=arm64
