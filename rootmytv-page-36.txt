Hmm, on newer LG CX firmwares, Calman can't connect to the internal
pattern generator? Also, why not use something like HCFR or Colourspace?
I heard Calman's 21 point 1D LUT could cause banding, and they have been
very aggressive with licencing. Colourspace does not share those
problems, licencing wise, has way less banding issues, and is much
cheaper, and no stupid yearly subscription for updates, or restriction
per TV brand. It can use pattern generator in your LG TV. The ZRO
licence lets you do only manual calibration, the higher licences let you
do 3DLUT....but unless you need that, the $30 ZRO is fine.
https://lightillusion.com/colourspace_zro.html Negative is are only
allowed to use colorspace on 1 pc, and move licence 5 times. And HCFR is
free, but like ZRO, manually, it however, is an excellent tool that
allows you precise calibration and verification for grayscale, color and
gamma. It also lets you l automatically display and measure grayscale to
fill the levels you have set in the view. However, it doesn't use LG's
internal pattern generator...which i personally think isn't really a
problem, just use PGenerator, you can use that with other devices as
well. After setting 1 source, click the button in picture mode settings,
color settings and white balance, to save to all other sources.
https://www.avsforum.com/threads/hcfr-open-source-projector-and-display-calibration-software.1393853/
Using HCFR and Colourspace, you can use Pgenerator (it's free) for your
grayscale patterns, and custom test patterns if you buy Tedd's ISO.
https://www.avsforum.com/threads/dedicated-raspberry-pi-pgenerator-thread-set-up-configuration-updates-special-features-general-usage-tips.3167475/
For both you can import spectral corrections other people made for a
specific TV model. Colourspace has a short form guide to give a general
idea how to manually calibrate your TV.
