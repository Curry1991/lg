02/19/2024 1:15 AM
A link and perhaps an addition to the paragraph about downgrades, that
these are generally problematic. Without a factory reset, you are almost
guaranteed to have an inconsistent system afterwards. The issue is now
even more delicate with the webOS upgrades. A number of conversions and
migrations are already carried out during the upgrade, which do not
always run smoothly. These migrations are generally not reversed during
the downgrade, and it is difficult to predict how the system will then
behave. In this context, perhaps an addendum to the firmware versions
from the test channels: these versions very often make experimental
changes to the system settings, which you can only get rid of cleanly by
performing a factory reset. This means that anyone who (seriously) tests
stuff with these versions usually carries out a factory reset when
switching to a stable version. Logically, LG does not always provide a
regular update path from an internal test version to a release version.

.kriilunaus 02/18/2024 7:36 PM
Since I have once again seen that incorrect information about the
current webOS firmware updates for the 2022 OLED models is being
distributed, a few comments in advance on similar questions that may be
asked here: 1.) The currently via LGE's regional support websites
distributed webOS 7 (22) USB update v04.40.91 cannot be installed on
devices which already have v04.40.90 installed. This is not a bug but
intentional! Devices updated to v04.40.90 have a different update ID as
a preparation for the upcoming webOS 23 upgrade. The update is purposely
rolled out for devices which haven't been updated to v04.40.90 yet. A
v04.40.91 update for devices with the new ID may follow soon, i.e.
there're currently two different v04.40.91 versions, one for the old IDs
and one for the new ones. Those updates differ slightly even though they
share the same version number. LGE may even decide to skip the v04.40.91
update for the devices which already have the new ID. 2.) The firmware
update v13.20.20 for the 2022 C2/G2 models is not "similar" to webOS 23,
it is the webOS 23 upgrade released by LG in South Korea at the end of
November 2023. The only difference is that version 13.20.20 has not been
released for regions other than South Korea and other regions have not
yet been supported. This will change with the upcoming webOS 23 upgrade,
which will be officially released for all regions. In addition, the
version is of course somewhat newer and includes a few additional
adjustments that have been developed since v13.20.20. (edited)

If you do not have any issues, you do not need to perform anything
manually. The compensation cycles are performed automatically at
specific time intervals after the television is switched off. This means
that, generally speaking, there are no advantages to starting the
process manually. There is also the following method to get the UTT
value, but I'm not sure if your model already supports it: Connect a USB
stick / drive to your TV and press the blue button on the remote control
seven times. Two text files are then created, one of which contains the
UTT value. There is also the option to enable the display of the usage
time for the European models in the normal settings menu. I think
ColorControl can even do this directly, otherwise Google will certainly
help you.
