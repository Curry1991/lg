I get why it sounds unusual if you are used to the traditional
download-and-store NAS setup! Instead of downloading media to a hard
drive, I use an app called Lampa MX on my webOS TV as my main interface
for movies and series, and I have it hooked up directly to both Prowlarr
and TorrServe. Here is how the workflow operates: Prowlarr runs in the
background and allows Lampa to search public trackers (like ThePirateBay
and YTS) directly from the TV. When I click on a movie to watch,
TorrServe catches the torrent file and streams it in real-time over the
network. Because I am just streaming the torrents directly, I don't need
a NAS for storage, and I don't need the rest of the *arr stack
(Sonarr/Radarr) to manage libraries. It creates a completely automated,
on-demand streaming platform right on the TV that is free forever.
