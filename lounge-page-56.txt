.kriilunaus 01/26/2024 9:42 PM
I know of one case where someone built such a Frankenstein TV with some
success. But you will definitely run into problems with such a
combination. To what extent and severity is not so easy to answer. The
MB decides, among other things, which system configurations are used,
which image parameters are set, etc. This means that without calibration
you will at least have sub-optimal image quality, at worst you will get
massive image errors or even crashes. There can also be a variety of
problems with the control and interaction with other components, e.g. if
PSU and T-CON are not meant to be used with the MB. To cut a long story
short, such a modification is not worthwhile except for technical
gimmicks or "scientific interest". If you want a functioning TV for
practical use, you should go for the original parts.
