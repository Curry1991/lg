IMO for best long-term support it'd be best to package your service as
an ipk
(https://webostv.developer.lge.com/develop/js-services/creating-js-services/),
make it register a service on the bus, and start it up by calling it in
init.d script (luna-send luna://your.service.name/xxx '{}') - you can
then configure your code so that it stays running in the background,
even after returning from that call.
05/15/2021 5:28 PM
"elevate" in the case of Homebrew Channel means patching up some Luna
Service Bus configurations, which makes our service (a piece of
