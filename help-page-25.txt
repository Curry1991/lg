For searchability purposes, I want to tell my solution to this problem.
My SJ800V (Firmware 6.10.55, WebOS 3.9) also showed the "missing
signature file" for start-devmode.sh in the autoroot.log – and the same
automatic uninstall of Homebrew on reboot – when trying the
dejavuln-autoroot method. The cause of this was very likely a previous
RootMy.TV exploit that had been patched out by a firmware update, but
had left a signature-less start-devmode.sh file on the device. While a
factory reset does help, I didn't have to go that far. I just installed
the Developer Mode app from the LG Content Store, started it once (this
was probably unnecessary) and immediately uninstalled it again, which
got rid of start-devmode.sh entirely. After that, the dejavuln-autoroot
process went through properly.
