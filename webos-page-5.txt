Common problem. Not sure where you are, but LG are aware of the problems
with their OLED PSUs and offer a free replacement in the UK. No idea if
this is also available elsewhere in the world. It is caused by the
yellow filter capacitors and 1 x ceramic power resistor on the power
supply. If left, they will fail and take out at least one of the
on-board fuses and one bank of the MOSFETs in that circuit. If you, or
someone you know, has the skills and knowledge of safely working with
PSUs, the parts are quite cheap. Do not attempt it if you do not know
about the dangers of working with PSUs.
