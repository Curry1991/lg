'G-SYNC Compatible' is essentially just a marketing term from nVidia, it
brings nothing to the table technologically. In other words, the
technology behind it is the normal VRR standards (HDMI 2 VRR etc). The
'GSYNC Compatible' label is a certification from nVidia. However, in
'real' G-SYNC monitors, a separate proprietary module from nVidia is
involved in the signal processing. As a result, the video connections of
G-SYNC monitors are always severely limited (usually one HDMI port and
one DP port) These modules often also need to be actively cooled.
