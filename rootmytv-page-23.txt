.kriilunaus 05/10/2025 2:24 PM
I assume you are referring to the NSU settings as found in the service
menu. The settings that can be found there are: NSU Mode, NSU Server,
NSU Search Image Mode, NSU Type If the root status of your TV or the
ability to root your TV is important to you, you should always stay away
from these settings. The probability of losing the ability to root the
TV or even the root state through such an update is significantly higher
than through a regular update (see FAQs). Beyond that, what I've always
said on the Channels applies here: unless you know exactly what you're
doing, stay away from these settings. You should be aware that you are
installing versions that LGE itself has not fully tested or that may
contain experimental changes. This little tested / untested state also
applies to the upgrade paths, where a lot can go wrong. (edited)
