yes, excatly this: root and install: 1. Enable Developer Mode on your TV
2. in DevMode Manager set up your TV and install homebrew channel 3. in
DevMode Manager go to terminal and execute touch
/var/log/crashd/"x;telnetd -l sh" The letter after "telnet -" is an
small "L"! 4. connect to your TV via telnet and execute
/media/developer/apps/usr/palm/services/org.webosbrew.hbchannel.service/elevate-service
to give root permissions to Homebrew Channel 6. rm -rf
/var/luna/preferences/devmode_enabled && mkdir -p
/var/luna/preferences/devmode_enabled 7. uninstall dev mode app 8.
Disable Quickstart+ in LG Settings 9. open piccap and configure:
hyperion host: 192.168.0.19 port: 19400 (default) autostart: enabled
Resolution Width: 256 Resolution Height: 144 capture method: new 2020+
gui + andere option: enabled -> Save -> Reboot TV !! Your should block
LG Updates with router entries, pi hole or similar !! (edited)
