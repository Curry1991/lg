slada1 03/09/2026 1:25 PM
ipk is just ordinary Debian package. You can unpack it and repack it
with ar and tar commands
    # unpack
    mkdir app_repack
    ar xvf original_ipk.ipk --output=app_repack
    cd app_repack
    # unpack data.tar.gz
    mkdir data
    tar -xvf data.tar.gz -C data
    rm data.tar.gz
