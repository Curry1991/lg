There is no official support from LG for USB Ethernet dongles. Some
dongles still work with a few limitations because the drivers are
available in the Linux kernel of webOS. However, you will not get the
full Gbit speed because of the limited USB2 ports (unless you've got a
model with a USB3 port). This means that in the end you will not get
significantly better speed than with a good WiFi connection, only the
stability of the connection may improve. No, there is no workaround
except to use a dongle whose drivers are available under webOS. I can't
provide you with a list right now, but a simple Google search should
give you user reports with working devices. (edited)
