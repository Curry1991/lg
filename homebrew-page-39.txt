My VU+ Duo4K satellite and cable receiver uses 2 2x FBC tuners for
DVB-S2X and DVB-C. Two FBC tuners are supplied via two satellite IF
inputs (LNB1 IN and LNB2 IN), then eight demodulators are dynamically
distributed to both FBC tuners. Each of these demodulators can then
demodulate a transponder or an SCR frequency. What are the advantages?
Faster switching times, as the next program can already be pre-tuned and
demodulated (so-called Fast Channel Change = FCC). In classic operation,
up to eight transponders could also be demodulated on one layer or, for
example, three from one layer and five from another if both satellite IF
inputs are supplied. Each demodulator could be assigned a Unicable/JESS
SCR frequency so that 8 transponders can be received independently of
each other. With a second tuner you get 16 independent transponders and
this is suitable for the ultimate SAT cable server.
