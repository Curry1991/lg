satdx62 01/03/2024 7:32 PM
The HDR Global switch turns on automatically when HyperHDR detects a
program or video in 4K and HDR or Dolby Vision. Forwarder and System
Screen capture do not need to be switched on in this case. A lag/delay
can also occur if you have selected too high a resolution in PicCap. The
lowest resolution is more than enough for the number of LEDs. Even 25fps
is enough. Sometimes it is also sufficient to select a low priority.
Instead of 150, use 100. You can see in Terminal with the command top
how the load on the CPU and memory goes when running the
“hyperion-webos” and “hyperhdr".
