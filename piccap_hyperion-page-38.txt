awawa_dev 05/15/2022 11:34 PM
Yes, TV latency is an important aspect. In the case of USB grabbers, it
is around 50-80 ms depending on the model and refresh&capture frequency.
This time includes image processing by HyperHDR. Need to add a few ms to
calculate LED colors and send data to LED. In my setup, the LED reacts
just before the TV (movie mode) and this effect can be recorded with the
phone in high frame rate mode. There may not be room for smoothing when
the image is captured on the TV. For smoothing, always the higher the
refresh rate the better, even when we exceed the USB capture frequency.
But we need to know our LED device capabilities (that can be measured)
and that's the only limitation. The smoothing delay can be decreased in
the processing configuration. The effect of turning off smoothing is
very unpleasant for me, probably low tolerance for blinking lights.
