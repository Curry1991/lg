For anyone in the US who tried jsbro and got stuck at "pending" - it was
a bug. The download manager on ATSC firmware rejects data: URIs with
errorCode -7 and redirects downloads to /media/internal/downloads
instead of /tmp. Submitted a PR with a fix that hosts the payload files
as static assets and updates the jsserver path to match. Confirmed
working on OLED55C4PUA firmware 33.31.20. If you're on US firmware and
thought you were patched, try again once this merges:
https://github.com/raws0kil/jsbro-autoroot/pull/1 (edited)
