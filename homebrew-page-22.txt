mr.meorzipan 07/25/2024 12:44 PM
For that very first update notification after a cold boot, you can only
block these by blocking LG's domains in your network, so the TV can't
reach specific domains from LG. After Homebrew Channel ran the script
though and you left 'Quick Start' turned on, on your TV (essentially
tells the TV to go into sleep mode when you turn off TV normally), any
subsequent update notification afterward won't come up again, until the
TV does a cold boot for whatever reason. (edited)
[mr.meorzipan]
[] itsrealm11c okay.
mr.meorzipan 07/25/2024 12:43 PM
As for update notifications, these can be blocked using that 'Block
System Updates' switch, this overlays the /etc/hosts file with adresses
that tell the TV to block some of LG's servers. However, since Homebrew
Channel (and thus it's scripts) starts later than the update checking
service, whenever you cold boot the TV (with a restart, crash of system,
power cable out and in, etc), you may see a update notification if an
firmware update is available, cause at that early point the scripts
hadn't been ran yet to block that one. (edited)
