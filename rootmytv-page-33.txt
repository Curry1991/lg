Doesn't have to to be eye bleedingly bright, you can set the same
brightness as C models without the MLA and heatsink, but G3 can reach
higher color luminance so it'll reach a satisfsctory looking brightness
with ease without being pushed hard or possibly looking as gray'ish in
some colors that can't reach 100cd/m or higher. For SDR, setting OLED
Light to value 1-3 the TV will set SDR whitelevel to 100cd/m, and for
HDR, the gray levels should still be mapped at specific luminance levels
(meaning it won't look any different), but for higher gray levels it'll
reach the correct luminance goals better. It'll take longer to have
burn-in issues as compared to C series. Due to higher white luminance of
above 1000cd/m, you'll get more pop, whereas 750-950cd/m would barely
reaches what you need. If you are in a bright room and set a brighter
picture mode to stop reflections, the TV can be brighter without looking
grayer as you increase OLED light, but chances are a lower brightness
than expected may do, since it might be a a bit better at stopping
reflections. (edited)
