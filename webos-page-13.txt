This is a question that only Paramount+ support can answer properly. But
from experience with other streaming services: Paramount+ had no UHD /
HDR / 5.1 content in Europe until recently. Now that they have made this
possible, it will take some time for all 1080p / stereo content to be
available in UHD / HDR / with 5.1 sound and there is no guarantee that
all old content will be converted and if so, how long it will take. So
the first thing you need to do is make sure that the content you want to
play is available in your region in UHD with DV and 5.1 sound. We had
the same situation in the past with Amazon Prime Video, where DV content
was sometimes only available in certain regions, so it wasn't a
technical limitation, but due to the source material delivered. As all
webOS streaming players use the TV's native APIs, no app update is
actually necessary for you to play DV content correctly. Of course, this
does not rule out the possibility that the P+ app may have bugs that
prevent this.
