Well... I wouldn't recommended trying to overclock the SoC in a TV
you're actually using. First of all, it would increase the SoC's
temperature when heat is already a problem. Second, you'd be messing
with like PLL control registers and might screw other stuff up. For
example, you might break communication between the CPU and video
peripherals. So there would be a lot of testing involved. And
theoretically you could permanently damage something (although atm I
can't think of a mechanism other than excessive heat or voltage). (By
the way, voltage and current limits are also software-controlled.) So
you'd be much better off experimenting on a dev board first.
