WebOS that firmware version is for, i think it's for an LG C1 with
WebOS6? Try Dejavu-Autoroot, it's linked in the faq. As for blocking the
update prompt, after rooting your TV and the Homebrew Channel is
installed and the TV rebooted, you can block updates and prompts that
appear, by enabling an option in the settings of Homebrew Channel, this
would block some of LG's domains the update checker uses to check or
download firmwares...however, this will not block the very first update
prompt that appears when you first start the TV on a cold boot (after
plugging out/in the power cable, or doing a real restart). Reason is
because the script of homebrew channel starts just a little later than
the service that prompts you for the update. But as long as you have
Quick Start enabled (is enabled by default), whenever you turn off the
TV with your remote, you do the equivalent of making the TV do Suspend
to Ram (or Deep Sleep or S3 power state, or whatever you wanna call it),
so when you wake the TV, it quickly starts and resumes where it left
off, so most of the time(unless the TV crashes or gets stuck for some
reason, and needs to reboot itself) the TV won't do a cold boot and you
won't see the update prompt. To stop that update prompt even on cold
boot or real restart, you have to block the domains the TV checks for
new firmwares, in your network, for example in your router, or some
computer (like a raspberri Pi or Intel NUC, or whatever) running adguard
home or pihole, or some DNS server you host within your network. The
domains you need to block are listed in the FAQ, here
https://discord.com/channels/407937994037919756/926881570512572456/1193643093174534254
