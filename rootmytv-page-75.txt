included with the homebrew channel app, dropbear, is looking for
(libcrypt.so.2 vs. libcrypt.so.1). please test the attached dropbear,
which is patched to try loading both versions of libcrypt. (replace
dropbear in

but keep a backup.) original workaround: 1. put a copy of libcrypt.so.1
from older webos (libcrypt.so.1.1.0 attached) somewhere writable: e.g.,
/media/developer/apps/usr/palm/services/org.webosbrew.hbchannel.service/lib
(which you'll have to create) 2. make a symlink to it named
