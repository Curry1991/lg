.pclin 06/22/2022 11:50 PM
I don't have any comparison pictures, they were previously different
installations. At 0 degrees 50% (LED typically 120 degrees) of the light
goes behind the TV at 30 degrees only 30% so it gets brighter and there
is a stronger effect around the TV. The larger the angle, distance and
number of LEDs/m, the fewer details are displayed because brighter LEDs
then outshine dark ones more. 30 degrees and a distance of 8 to 10 cm
with 60 LED/m is the best compromise for me because I like to have
details and not just colorful surfaces. 30 degrees also has the
advantage that you can bend the LED stripes well and no soldering is
required, the LEDs are then also in the corners with a small distance.
