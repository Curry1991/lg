07/10/2026 12:59 AM
LG uses a proprietary hardware demultiplexer called SDEC (essentially
the component that takes the raw broadcast multiplex from the tuner and
splits it into the individual video, audio and metadata streams), and a
PVR subsystem that’s normally used for recording live TV. The approach
I’ve taken is to create a second SDEC channel that mirrors the currently
tuned channel, configure it with the same packet IDs as the active
programme, and route its output into an unused PVR download channel.
Instead of writing an encrypted recording to disk like LG normally does,
a standalone userspace program reads the transport stream directly from
the PVR download buffer before any recording-specific processing
happens. That proof of concept is now working: it can stream the
original MPEG transport stream over the network into VLC while the TV
continues displaying the channel (or some other HDMI input) normally,
without injecting into LG’s TV process.
