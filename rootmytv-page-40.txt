specifically for the webOS 23 upgrade. Various migrations take place
during the upgrade process, which can go wrong and leave “remnants”
behind. As you can see, especially in the forums, this can have
different effects. From lost settings, to settings that cannot be
changed, to problems with eARC and other device features. A factory
reset fixes many of these problems. Of course, this is all without
taking into account the fact that you also lose root access as a result
of the factory reset. Which you can get back with “DejaVuln”.
