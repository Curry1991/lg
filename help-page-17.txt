Depending on the webOS version (old or new), different libraries are
required for UI and video capturing (hyperion-webos). Even within the
same webOS version, the libraries for UI and video capturing differ. As
automatic detection does not always work, the correct backend version
must be forced for older webOS. Some TV models are compatible with a
specific backend, but require a slightly different routine to work
reliably. This is exactly where the quirks come into play. If you
absolutely need the UI capturing function, you can activate it in
PicCap. To do this, select the “libgm” backend or change “config.json”
from "uibackend":"auto" to "uibackend":"libgm" and "nogui":true in
"nogui":false. This will probably work, but the CPU utilization of your
device will be significantly higher. I don't know whether the set FPS
rate will still be achieved. You can find the corresponding explanation
here: https://github.com/webosbrew/hyperion-webos#backends You can also
find useful tips in my guide. See:
https://github.com/satgit62/How-to-Install-and-Set-Up-Ambilight-on-LG-webOS
