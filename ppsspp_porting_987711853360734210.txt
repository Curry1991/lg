It didn't look for shared libraries, apparently, only static ones. There
is the proper ffmpeg version in the ppsspp source tree. Just compile
that, set --prefix to something quick and just do a make install -j <N>
. Then, when in PPSSPP, use -DFFMPEG_DIR= and specify where you
"installed" the proper ffmpeg. It'll then get picked up.
