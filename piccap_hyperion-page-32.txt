It depends on the LEDs stripes used and the controller used. What LEDs
and controllers are they using? With 4-channel RGBW LEDs, you can select
the right LEDs in WLED under LED Preferences. Color Order is GBR and
White Balance correction, and select Dual for “Auto-calculate white
channel from RGB”. Then the white channel also lights up with the white.
In Hyperion/HyperHDR, Color Order is RGB. You can also play with the
respective color in Hyperion with the RGB gamma value until the colors
of the LEDs match the picture. 1=Neutral above 1 color is removed and
below 1 color is added. (edited)

[] xxcyanidexx How to setup width and height correctly in piccap?
satdx62 09/05/2022 5:17 PM
Hi, The common resolutions for PicCap are: 128x72 160x90 192x108 256x144
360x180 I use 192x108. The width and height in the app has nothing to do
with the real setting of your TV. If the resolution is set too high in
the app, the light from the LEDs may be delayed or not appear at all
compared to the image. Just test what is best for you.

shadow0304. 08/31/2022 5:40 PM
USB delivers 5v at maybe 2A, ESP32 steps it down to 3V. Since ESP32 is
not designed to deliver voltage through vin (even though it works,
you're supposed to supply power to it from there in lieu of USB), trying
to pull more than about 1-1.5A from it starts to wear on the components
of ESP32. With 120 LED's, you can pull a maximum of 36W or 7A. You can
see the issue is that you're underpowering them vs their full potential.
Do you need a 5V 7A PSU? probably not, but your setup is certainly
wearing on your ESP32 just like mine did last year. (edited)
