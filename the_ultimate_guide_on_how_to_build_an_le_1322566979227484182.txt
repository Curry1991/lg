Hi, You can use an external USB grabber to grab the image from your
external devices (SAT-Cable Receiver, Sony PlayStation, Fire
TV-Stick/Cube or Apple TV) and send it to Hyperion or HyperHDR on
Raspberry Pi. This can then control various LED controllers so that you
can also enjoy Ambilight. See:
https://www.hyperhdr.eu/2023/02/ultimate-guide-on-how-to-build-led.html
You can also wait until a new exploit is released for your device and
follow these instructions:
https://github.com/satgit62/How-to-Install-and-set-up-Ambilight-on-LG-webOS
Most LED controllers and the Hyperion/HyperHDR settings are also
suitable for an external solution.
