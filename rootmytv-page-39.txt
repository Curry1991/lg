method needed for Crashd and WTA methods. If you want something that

might bring that to other models too, so, a tip, please turn off 'Auto
Update' in the Software Update menu, if you have it on, you should be
the one in control when to update the firmware, not the TV, and stop
updating your firmware if you want to keep using firmware
vulnerabilities to root your TV, unless you know for sure a firmware
doesn't patch things. And after rooting, do not update to a firmware
that patched a rooting method as well, cause if you ever lose Homebrew
Channel or root access and the permanent dev mode that the rooting
method enabled for you, you can't get it back (until the next rooting
method is discovered someday). Things like factory reset, or some error
or whatever could cause a factory reset and thus you may need that
vulnerability to root it again. (edited)
