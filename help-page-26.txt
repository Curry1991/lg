.kriilunaus 12/21/2024 4:25 PM
I forgot to answer this question: RS compensation adjusts the power
supply to each pixel to compensate for the threshold shift of each
pixel's TFT. These shifts occur relatively quickly (depending on the
scenario, within minutes or hours) and have nothing directly to do with
wear and tear on the OLED materials. The JB compensation adjusts the
pixels to each other based on the different wear across the entire
panel. This therefore compensates for the inevitable ageing of the OLED
materials. (edited)
