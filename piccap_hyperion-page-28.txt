satdx62 10/24/2022 5:16 PM
You can achieve the color calibration by adjusting the gamma values ​​so
that on your background wall the same color spectrum is displayed as on
the television picture. For understanding: Gamma red The gamma of red.
1.0 is neutral. Over 1.0 it reduces red, lower than 1.0 it adds red.
Gamma green The gamma of green. 1.0 is neutral. Over 1.0 it reduces
green, lower than 1.0 it adds green. Gamma blue The gamma of blue. 1.0
is neutral. Over 1.0 it reduces blue, lower than 1.0 it adds blue. By
adjusting these values, you can achieve true-to-life color rendering, so
yellow is really yellow and not green. Under Remote Control of
Hyperion/HyperHDR, by changing these values, you can immediately see in
the image whether the adjustment was successful. If this is the case,
you can then permanently change and save under Image Processing. See
pictures. Specifically in my case, in order to get a real blue on the
wall, I reduced the blue gamma to 1 and now I also have a real native
blue to the TV picture on the wall. So you have to set the gamma values
​​so that everything fits. It really isn't rocket science. (edited)
