can't play DTS, then an computer or Nvidia Shield 2019 (would support
Dolby Vision) connected to the AVR will offer you more. The AVR would
then be able to receive the full bandwidth, not limited by ARC, and also

passthrough DTS. You may even be able to play more video and audio

Shield 2019 (or other device that suffices) to AVR seems cheaper than
