performance and has none impact on delay because neopixel renderers in

performance stability a bit, less incomplete frames thanks to parallel
processing buffer/serial port. But we are talking about loss of 0-2 per
80 frames/second before especially using 4Mb speed (serial buffer may
not be process in time).
