[webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop] New comment on issue #131: TIMED OUT
There are few possibilities that you get this timed out error: 1. Your
TV obtained another address and the one in the manager app can't be
reached anymore 2. Developer mode has been turned off, either by its
timer or manually. 3. Your TV and the computer aren't located in the
same network. Rather than reinstalling the app, I suggest you remove the
TV in the app and add it again. If you see any dialogs showing up during
the process, there are some issues happening probably.
