stands for Power Factor Correction, it's usually accompanied by what
kind, like simulated sine wave and pure sine wave. Maybe something just
says 'sinewave', but that's not useful. If your computer's power supply
has Active PFC feature, which was mandated in the EU for decreasing

to 'unstable' or simulated sinewave, it may be under stress, make coil
whine sound, some might even just suddenly shut down out of nowhere.

sine wave PFC, but i'd just be safe and just use a UPS with pure sine
wave PFC. (edited)
