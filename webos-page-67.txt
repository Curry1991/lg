Fully fixed by replacing the root cert. 1. download
https://letsencrypt.org/certs/isrgrootx1.pem.txt 2. save the txt file
somewere on the tv storage, e.g. create a folder for it in
/media/cryptofs/root/ 3. add a mount bind to
/usr/share/ca-certificates/mozilla/DST_Root_CA_X3.crt in your
/media/cryptofs/apps/usr/palm/services/com.palmdts.devmode.service/start-devmode.sh
file. (e.g.
mount -o bind /media/cryptofs/root/ca-certificates/isrgrootx1.pem.txt /usr/share/ca-certificates/mozilla/DST_Root_CA_X3.crt)
4. reboot, everything should work again (edited)
