mr.meorzipan 09/12/2024 8:53 PM
I've move a 65" LG OLED TV a few times. I first wrapped the TV with
plastic foil, then with 2 layers of bed blankets, and some additional
cushioning on the 4 edges, wrapped tape around the TV horizontally and
vertically to keep bed blankets in place so no holes can appear
anywhere. It'll give some protection against scratching, and maybe very
minor impact, but you still need to carefully handle it. Then make sure
to have a car ready that can accomodate the size of thr TV, placing a
lot of cushioning and as flat and spread out as possible to give equal
cushioning on the entire screen, and also add lots of cushioning on the
edges, and if the TV can't lay flat down, the, then diagonally, but in
that case, must and make sure no things exist that puncure into the TV
while it stands diagonally, and be carefully of the windows or ceiling,
place a lot of thick cushioning to keep it in place so it does not fold
in 2 under it's own weight due to heavy vibration of the car. Then
carefully moves TV with 2 people, planning the entire path how to move
the TV safely carry it (or if the door and path could even fit that 65"
TV), constantly without err looking for anything the TV could bump into.
For example, when walking down stairs, look if TV might bump against the
ceiling, or touch the walls when going a corner. And look if friend is
becoming tired or you're walking too slow or fast. Plan who walks in
front or in the back, and whoever is stronger and has more stamina
should hold more weight. For the mountains, watch for bumpy roads, you
want to minimize heavy vibrations on the car for TV. If the road is very
steep, make sure car can handle the weight when going up roads. Never
move it alone, and if possible, do not move it with someone that tires
easily or gets stressed out fast where they become incapable of being
rational and calm. Always keep attention on the TV when moving in case
of unexpected irregularities, even in the car (as passenger of course),
and drive careful. (edited)
