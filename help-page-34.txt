    Widevine L2 is actually an older, deprecated level of Widevine DRM.
    In the past, Widevine had four levels of security:
    1. Widevine L1: The highest level of security, which provides the most robust protection for premium content.
    2. Widevine L2: A lower level of security than L1, but still provided robust protection for HD and SD content.
    3. Widevine L3: An even lower level of security than L2, which was used for lower-quality content or legacy devices.
    4. Widevine Modular (L3): This is the same as Widevine L3, but with a more modular architecture that allows for easier integration and customization.
    However, Widevine L2 is no longer used or supported by Google, and it's been deprecated in favor of the more modern and secure Widevine L1 and L3 levels.
    Today, you'll mostly see devices and services supporting either Widevine L1 or Widevine L3 (or Modular), but not L2.
