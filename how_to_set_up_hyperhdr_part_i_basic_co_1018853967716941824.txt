Explanation HyperHDR's LUT table is used for HDR to SDR tone mapping
when enabled in the grabber settings. ALSO THE LUT TABLE IS USED FOR RGB
GAMMA CHANNELS CORRECTION AND MINIMAL TRESHOLD FOR ALL YUV (YUV, NV12,
I420) TO RGB STREAM DECODERS EVEN WITHOUT TONE MAPPING OPTION ENABLED.
It is recommended to capture a screeenshot from your grabber preview in
the browser (turn off hdr tone mapping first) to test settings on that
sample. After you finish creating new LUT table please upload new
'lut_lin_tables.3d' to your configuration folder. Your typical
configuration folder to upload that file in is (check 'Logs' page to
confirm): Rpi→/home/pi/.hyperhdr Windows→c:/Users/NAME/.hyperhdr
