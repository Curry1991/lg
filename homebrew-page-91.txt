I'm the guy who created the downmix DTS to stereo PCM patch for mkv
playback and at this stage I don't expect that there will ever be DTS
passthrough because most of the decoding/playback/passthrough appears to
be accomplished in closed source libraries that issue direct call to the
hardware, and the cost/benefit of reverse engineering something like
this is way too high. At this stage, the best I'm hoping can be
accomplished is DTS to multichannel PCM for mkv playback and that's
about it. So, if you are hoping for DTS passthrough for movie playback
or DTS passthrough to an amp from the HDMI inputs (which is a different
matter but also something that'd be handled in the closed source parts
of the firmware with reliance on hardware calls), you're most likely in
for a very long wait...
