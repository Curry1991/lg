the same issue - try setting Audio Amp in Tool Option3 to 'ntp7518 4amp'
to restore some bass, and then boost it further in the EQ settings if
required.
febman_21236 04/07/2026 4:53 PM
The most common cause of main boards going down (when not damaged by a
faulty PSU) is CPU to PCB connection failure. The BGA CPU is attached to
the PCB by small balls of solder (Ball Grid Array - BGA). Over time with
repeated heating up and cooling down, the (crappy) lead-free solder
balls expand and contract and some may make/break connection with the
PCB, leading to intermittent faults. The other main causes are
capacitors dying. Were there any signs prior to it giving up completely
or was it all of a sudden when one time you tried to power on the TV?

The setting used to control access to real service menus is named
svcMenuFlag. When it is true, you get the neutered menus. To enable the
full menus, run this as root:
    luna-send -n 1 'luna://com.webos.settingsservice/setSystemSettings' '{"category":"other","settings":{"svcMenuFlag":false}}'
(Make sure false is not quoted.) You can also call this Luna method via
any other privileged means of accessing the Luna bus (e.g.,
/createAlert). Keywords for garbage Discord search: service menu,
svcMenuFlag
