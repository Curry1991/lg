/var/lib/webosbrew/init.d/ with the name: cp210x.ko 2. insert the

the following command: chmod +x /var/lib/webosbrew/init.d/01-cp210x.ko

say. The alleged stopping of the recording in Piccap can have other
causes. Mostly it is due to a disturbed WI-FI/network connection, so
that the communication between Hyperion and ESP/WLED comes to a
standstill. In WLED settings under Wi-Fi Setup, disable Wi-Fi sleep.
Another cause is a higher CPU load at the moment when the recording
stops. Reduce the frames rate in Piccap to 20 and priority to 100. How
your CPU load is, you can find out via Terminal/SSH with the command
“top”. Furthermore, you have the possibility to localize the errors in
Hyperion/HyperHDR under Logs. Is that on the picture the IP address of
your Hyperion? Is Hyperion not installed on your TV, but externally?
Internally, the IP address should be 127.0.0.1 (Localhost).

You can flash WLED on ESP directly and easily via your Chrome or Edge
browser. Install the USB chip driver of your ESP, connect it via USB to
your PC and enter it into the browser https://install.wled.me/, and
follow the instructions. WLED software installation at:
https://wled-install.github.io/ You can connect via WI-FI if your WI-FI
signal is good, or via LAN if your ESP32 has LAN interface. There is
also a special software for ESP that allows fast communication via USB
interface. It is called HyperSerial and is designed by @Awawa to work
with HyperHDR. HyperSerial can be found on GitHub at:
https://github.com/awawa-dev/HyperSerialEsp8266 https://kno.wled.ge/ and
https://www.hyperhdr.eu/2023/02/ultimate-guide-on-how-to-build-led.html
