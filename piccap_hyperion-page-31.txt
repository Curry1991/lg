With 4-channel Sk6812 LEDs (RGBW), it makes sense to select White
Managements, White Balance Correction: on and “Dual”. Then, with the
white background, images are not converted from RGB, but the white
channel is used. The basic colors remain untouched. So green is really
green, and so are red and blue. The rest is calibrated in
Hyperion/HyperHDR, under gamma correction. Value 1 is neutral, more than
1 color is removed and below 1 color is added. In this way, you get some
dimensions adjusted for each wall color in the background. (edited)
