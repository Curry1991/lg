.kriilunaus 04/30/2026 9:32 PM
Generally speaking: you definitely should not try to force an update
that is not marked as compatible. In the worst case, you could brick
your TV. In the best case, even basic things might not work properly,
and you could end up with terrible picture or sound quality. Each model
has specific image parameters stored in the firmware, among other
things. If those are missing, the best-case scenario is that you end up
in some kind of fallback mode. Your only chance would be if LG did not
actually remove support for the model from the A2/B2 firmware, because
they were sloppy, and only blocked the update mechanism. But that would
need to be investigated more closely and would take a lot of time.
