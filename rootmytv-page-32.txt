[satdx62]
[] ravi7878 Does anyone know how HyperSPI works?
satdx62 12/17/2024 8:10 PM
HyperSPI is an SPI bridge for the AWA protocol to control an LED strip
of HyperHDR hosted on a Raspberry Pi. In this case, Raspberry Pi acts as
the master, and the LED controller ESP8266/ESP32/ESP32-S2/rp2040
(Raspberry Pi Pico) is in slave mode. This avoids a hardware limitation
of the Raspberry Pi design and thus Adalight is alternatively replaced
by a pure SPI data transmission, thus achieving a very fast data
transmission. Since an external USB grabber shares the data line with
the Adalight serial driver, this leads to a rather strong reduction of
the total USB transmission. Therefore, this bridge counteracts this and
creates the conditions for a fast data transfer. Since we do not need an
external grabber and Raspberry Pi for our PicCap/Hyperion solution,
HyperSPI is completely unsuitable. (edited)

therefore you have to re-root. This can be understood by the presence of
startup.sh from /var/lib/webosbrew/ before uninstalling HB and after.

directory. The root permissions are written to /var/luna-service2-dev/.
These must also be removed, or you delete them yourself. For PicCap and
Hyperion and other apps that need root, autostart entries are written in
