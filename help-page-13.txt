your computer (I use SoftEther VPN Client Manager and Connectify Hotspot

with the TV via a LAN cable). You only need to do this once. 2. Connect
the TV, you should get access, and the message that the application is
blocked in your country should disappear, and you should be able to open
all applications normally. 3. Now you can disable VPN on your PC, TV
access will remain even if you connect to your regular internet (access
will remain until you restart/turn off the LG). 3. Go to your router
settings, Parental Controls for your TV, and block access to the
following DNS list: lge.com *.lge.com lgappstv.com *.lgappstv.com
lgtvcommon.com *.lgtvcommon.com lgtvsdp.com *.lgtvsdp.com webos.lge.com

worry if the internet settings show that the TV is not fully connected
to the internet, this is how it should be. (edited)
