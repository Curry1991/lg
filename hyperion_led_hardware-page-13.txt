ANY video source including piccap, you must count the LEDs horizontally
and vertically, video source width/height respectively should be at
least x2. e.g. if top edge is 1.3 meter then for 144LED/meter video
source width should have at least 375 pixels, preferable even higher.

[awawa_dev]
awawa_dev 01/21/2023 11:18 AM
You should also provide higher resolution video source than for LED
strips of lower density. Otherwise you could experience very unpleasant
"the pixelize" effect on LEDs especially when something on the video
source is moving. Video width/height per single LED aspect should be al
least 2 or greater, as workaround you can increase size of LED area for
color computing on the image from video source but it means more
blending and loss of details and the saturation. (edited)

If the live video flickers at 256x144 and this is visible on the LEDs,
this is not normal. The problem is probably with your LG/PicCap. Or on
your browser where you look at a scaled preview as @asturel already
wrote. Hyperion calculates the colors and brightness for an LED from an
image area. If this was scaled 'cleanly' beforehand, there is no
difference in the result on the LEDs, but Hyperion has to calculate
significantly fewer pixels. The SoC CPUs are too weak for such
calculations with UHD images. (edited)

0x10000 firmware_esp32_s2_mini_SK6812_RGBW_NEUTRAL.bin

awawa_dev 12/24/2022 10:53 AM
Hi It's ESP S3 and it's not supported yet in HyperSerialESP32. We use
little processor power, so CPU power is secondary matter. The stability
of the serial connection and the quality of workmanship are important.
And the number of I2S but most likely it will change soon and the ESP32
S2 Lolin mini will also be recommended if we want to use the new
HyperSerialESP32 function of multisegments. In the case of CDC (ESP32 S2
mini) we still have to wait with the assessment of stability and
compatibility with various systems, but the price of ~ $ 2 is very
attractive. ESP32 MH ET LIVE is a harvester that will handle almost
everything and is well made, but more than 2 times more expensive.
Photos of these models are on the HyperSerialESP32 project page, with
others you will have to experiment on your own. (edited)
