ryman.1 03/15/2024 3:45 PM
hey @hellobox @kitsuned, I just wanted to follow up on this from a few
days ago. I got into the weeds and figured out exactly what works for
generating long presses via LG IR. I confirmed LG uses NEC encoding
https://www.sbprojects.net/knowledge/ir/nec.php (thanks for providing
this resource, @hellobox ). The IR receiver also follows the NEC
"repeat" protocol, which is then used to calculate long presses. Rapidly
retransmitting the complete code, rather than using "repeat" signals, is
not a means to generate long presses. It appears that the minimum
duration of a long press detection is determined on a per-software
basis. For WebOS Home, a long press is detected after ~9 or 10 NEC
repeat cycles (~1 second). For YouTube app it's a shorter 5 NEC repeat
cycles (~500ms) for a long press. This capture is from a successful
Enter/OK long press. The signal I captured signal is visually inverted,
so don't let the confuse you.
