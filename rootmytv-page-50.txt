hellobox. 09/19/2023 9:12 AM
for everyone having SSL issues with rootmy.tv and the webosbrew repo,
the cause is Cloudflare switching to Let's Encrypt certs as of a few
days ago (you now have to pay to get a non-LE certificate). if your TV
is already rooted and you want to remain on old firmware, you should add
the LE CA cert manually (e.g.
https://github.com/RootMyTV/RootMyTV.github.io/issues/9#issuecomment-936337656)
if RootMyTV runs into SSL issues, you can try https://rootmytv.inf.re/
(different CA). some potentially useful information:
https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel/issues/62#issuecomment-1724495306
